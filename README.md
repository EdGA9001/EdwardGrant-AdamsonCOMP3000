Late bird (COMP3000 Project)

Allocated supervisor: Girdhar (Was Vassilis)

#Project vision:

Late bird is an automatically adjusting alarm clock that aims to improve on existing alarm clocks by focusing on both ends of sleep inertia. Both the waking side - ensuring the user stays awake. As well as the sleeping side - further reducing sleep enertia and helping them maintain a minimum level of rest in circumstances like jet lag where sleep may have been disrupted.

Given good rest and reliable waking times, the projects aims to help users make the most of morning hours while also allowing them to pull out the stops to get an extra sleep cycle (roughly 90 minutes) to ensure the late bird gets the worm throughout the day.
